# Favorite Series: Chasing in The Wild

## Summary of the Series:
"Chasing in the Wild" is a 2024 Philippine teen romantic drama television series that follows the story of Sevi, a basketball captain from a humble family, and Elyse, a wealthy and spoiled cheerleader. Despite their seemingly opposite backgrounds, they fall in love and must navigate the challenges of their young love as they face harsh realities together. The series explores themes of friendship, rich kids, and relationships, with a focus on the characters' personal growth and the impact of their love on their lives.
